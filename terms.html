1. What the app is

Kuttu Mobile is a phone app for garment businesses (factories, wholesalers, showrooms, tailors and boutiques). Shop staff use it for stitching orders, measurements, billing, stock, dues, staff attendance and reports. It is provided by Kuttu, Hyderabad, India. The app is free to download from Google Play, but it works only with a shop account (section 2).

2. Shop accounts and who may use them

  • No public sign-up. Kuttu sets up a shop and its Owner login after the shop buys Kuttu. Staff then sign in with the shop code, a user ID and a password.
  • Three roles. The Owner can see and manage everything in the shop. A Supervisor sees orders, stock and attendance but not money. A Worker sees only the orders assigned to them and their own attendance.
  • The Owner is responsible for the shop's users. The Owner decides who gets a login and what role they have, and must remove a login when a person leaves.
  • Adults only. The app is for people aged 18 and over.
  • Keep sign-in details private. Do not share a password. Anything done with a login is treated as done by that user. Tell us at once if a password may be known to someone else.

3. Plans and payment

  • The plans, prices and offers are the ones shown on kuttu.org when the shop buys. A plan is for the period stated when you buy.
  • Payment is made to Kuttu directly, not through Google Play, and the app has no buying buttons. We confirm your payment with you before we set up the shop.
  • Refunds, renewals and the way offers can change or end follow the Terms and Conditions on the website.

4. What the shop agrees to do

  • Enter true and lawful details, and only the customer and staff details the shop needs.
  • Have the right to use the customer and staff details it puts into the app, and tell the people concerned that it keeps them.
  • Keep its own copy of anything important, such as bills and orders. The app is a working tool, not a legal archive.
  • Use the app only for its own garment business.

5. Attendance and location

The app records staff attendance with the phone's location. While a Worker or Supervisor is on shift (from Punch In to Punch Out, but not during a break), it records where the phone is from time to time, even when the app is closed, and tells the Owner and Supervisor if the person moves more than the set distance (1 km) from the office. The details are in section 3 of the privacy policy.

  • The shop decides to use attendance tracking and must tell its employees before they use it, and must follow the laws that apply to employee monitoring. Kuttu provides the tool only.
  • An employee who does not allow location cannot punch in, because attendance depends on it.
  • Attendance and location may be used by the shop only to manage work and attendance, and must not be used to follow a person outside their shift.
