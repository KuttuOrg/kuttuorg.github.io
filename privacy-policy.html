Kuttu Mobile: privacy policy

Last updated 22 November 2026 · Applies to the Kuttu Mobile app for Android

Kuttu Mobile is a phone app for garment businesses (factories, wholesalers, showrooms, tailors and boutiques). Shop staff use it for stitching orders, measurements, billing, stock, dues and attendance. This policy explains what the app stores, who can see it, and how to have it removed. It is written in plain words; if anything is unclear, write to us (section 12).

This policy covers the mobile app. How the kuttu.org website and the Kuttu desktop products handle details is in the privacy note on the website.

1. Who is responsible

Kuttu, Hyderabad, India, provides the app and the cloud service behind it. Each shop that uses the app is set up by Kuttu after the shop buys Kuttu, and there is no public sign-up.

  • The shop decides what customer and staff details it enters, and why. Its records belong to the shop, not to Kuttu.
  • Kuttu stores those records for the shop and looks after the service. We use them only to run the app for that shop (section 4). Kuttu is directly responsible for the sign-in details of the app's users.

3. Location and attendance

  • When location is read. The phone's location is read when an employee taps Punch In, and then from time to time, including while the app is closed or the phone is locked, until they tap Punch Out. Employees can tap Break Start and Break End during a shift; no location is read between Break Start and Break End, and reading resumes at Break End. Before Punch In and after Punch Out the app does not read location. This applies to Workers and Supervisors. The shop Owner's own punch reads one position and is not tracked afterwards.
  • What is stored. The time and position of each reading (a movement trail) for the shift, how far each was from the shop's saved office location, and whether it was inside the shop's set distance. The Owner and Supervisor can see the trail.
  • The 1 km alert. If an employee on shift moves more than the shop's alert distance (set at 1 km) from the office, the app sends a notification to the Owner and Supervisor of that shop, saying who and when.
  • You will see it happening. While location is being recorded on a shift, the phone shows a notification saying so. The app asks for the permission first, with an explanation, including the “Allow all the time” choice that Android requires for background location. Attendance needs location: an employee who does not allow it cannot punch in.
  • Who sees it. Only the Owner and Supervisor of that shop see everyone's attendance and trail. A Worker sees only their own.
  • The shop's duty. The shop decides to use attendance tracking and must tell its employees. Kuttu provides the tool.

4. How we use it

  • To run the app for the shop: sign-in, orders, billing, stock, attendance, reminders and reports.
  • To record attendance and to tell the shop's Owner and Supervisor when an employee on shift moves away from the office (section 3).
  • To keep the service working and safe: backups, fixing faults and preventing misuse.
  • To reply when you write to us for help.

We do not use shop or customer records for advertising, and we do not look at them except when a shop asks us for help or when we must investigate a fault or misuse.

5. Who can see it

Records are kept apart for each shop. A user of one shop cannot read another shop's data. Inside a shop, what a person sees depends on the role:

  • Owner: everything in the shop, including money, staff and attendance.
  • Supervisor: orders, stock and attendance, but not money.
  • Worker: only the orders assigned to them, and their own attendance.

Customer phone numbers and prices are not shown to Workers. Attendance and the movement trail are visible to the Owner and Supervisor, and to the employee themselves (section 3).

6. Who else handles it

  • Google Firebase (Google LLC) provides the sign-in service, the database, photo storage and the background functions the app runs on. It processes the data for us, under Google's terms.
  • WhatsApp: when a user taps a WhatsApp button, the app hands a ready message to the user's own WhatsApp. The message is sent from the user's own account. We do not read it.
  • The law: we may give details to the authorities when the law requires it.

We do not sell the data and we do not share it for advertising.

7. How it is kept safe

  • Data travels between the phone and the cloud over encrypted connections.
  • Access rules, checked on the server, keep each shop's data apart and enforce the roles in section 5.
  • Passwords are never stored in readable form by us.
  • Records are never erased from the phone side; deleting an order marks it deleted so that the change can sync.

No system is perfectly safe. If we learn of a problem that affects your data, we will tell the shop's Owner.

8. How long we keep it

  • While a shop is active, we keep its records so that it can keep working.
  • When a shop is closed or asks to be deleted, the shop is suspended first. If nobody reactivates it, its records, photos and exports are permanently deleted after a waiting period of 30 days, and its staff sign-ins are removed. The shop code is kept reserved so that it is never reused.
  • Movement trails (the positions recorded during a shift) and the distance alerts raised from them are deleted automatically after 90 days. The punch-in and punch-out times stay as the attendance record.
  • Backup copies of the database are made regularly and may remain for a short time after deletion before they are overwritten.
  • Administration logs (who did what, and when, to a shop's account) are kept for the service's records.

9. Deleting your account and data

To delete your account or your shop's data, write to us:

  • Email enquiry@kuttu.org or message +91 72076 29246 on WhatsApp.
  • Give your shop code, your user ID and say whether you want your own login or the whole shop deleted.
  • We may ask a question only the account's owner can answer before we act.
  • A staff member (Supervisor or Worker) asks the shop's Owner to remove their login, or writes to us. Records they created for the shop, such as orders and attendance, stay with the shop unless the shop asks for them to be removed.
  • An Owner can ask for the whole shop to be deleted. It follows the 30-day waiting period in section 8, and the Owner can ask for a copy of the shop's data first.
  • A customer of a shop whose details are in the app should ask the shop. If the shop cannot help, write to us and we will pass the request on.

10. Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the details we hold about you, withdraw permissions in the phone's settings, and complain to us if you think your details were mishandled. We handle personal data in line with the laws of India, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Write to us (section 12) and we will reply.

11. Children

Kuttu Mobile is a business tool for adults and is not meant for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect details from children.
