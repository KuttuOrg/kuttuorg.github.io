How it works

1. Message us

Tell us what you sell and which city or area you serve.

2. We set up your listing

Send your logo, photos and a short description. We write and place the advert for you.

3. Enquiries come to you

Shop owners who tap Call or WhatsApp reach you directly. You pay monthly by UPI.

4. See the results

After the first 3 months we send you a short report of visits and enquiries, so you can decide whether to continue.

Our promises

Paid ads are marked

Every paid item shows as Sponsored, so shop owners can trust the site.

No shop data is shared

We never give advertisers a Kuttu user's records or contact list. You only receive enquiries that people send you themselves.

No lock-in

Pay month to month and stop whenever you like.

Questions

Can I advertise if I am not in Hyderabad?

Yes. Tell us which cities you serve and we list you for those areas.

What can I advertise?

Products and services for the garment trade. We do not accept adverts that are misleading, or for businesses outside the trade.

How do I pay?

Monthly by UPI. We send you a simple invoice and a receipt.

Do you promise a number of enquiries?

No. We will not promise sales, and we will not quote numbers we cannot show. You get a real report of visits and enquiries and you decide.
