Careers

Build software for real shops.

Kuttu is a small team making software that garment businesses use every day. We mostly look for interns, and our roles are usually part-time, so students and early-career applicants are very welcome. If you like practical problems and seeing your work used at a counter, we'd like to hear from you.

Interns preferred · Part-time roles welcome

Sales · Hyderabad

Sales Executive

Help garment businesses discover software that makes their work easier. You will meet shop owners, wholesalers, factory owners or managers, boutique owners and tailors, understand how they run their business, and show them how our software can help through demos in person, over the phone or on WhatsApp. You will follow up with leads, answer questions honestly, explain pricing clearly, and help each customer decide on the edition that fits their shop. You will also keep a simple record of your leads and meetings, and share what customers ask for with our team. We are looking for someone who is confident and friendly in conversation, comfortable learning a product well enough to demonstrate it, and able to speak with customers in Telugu, Hindi or English. Experience in sales or in the garment or retail trade is a plus. Freshers with good communication skills are welcome. Tell us about yourself and the languages you speak.

Marketing · Hyderabad

Marketing Manager (4 to 5 years' experience)

Help garment businesses across India find out that Kuttu exists, and understand why it suits them. You will plan and run our marketing for factories, wholesalers, showrooms, tailors and boutiques: our website, Instagram and Facebook pages, WhatsApp messages, Google search and paid ads, paper ads and pamphlets, and visits to markets and trade events. We are looking for someone with 4 to 5 years of marketing experience, ideally in retail, fashion, textiles or business software, who writes clearly, understands social media and Google ads, is comfortable with numbers, and can speak with shop owners in Telugu, Hindi or English. Tell us about campaigns you have run, the results you achieved, and the languages you speak.

Engineering · Hyderabad

QA Engineer (Manual Testing)

Make sure our software works properly before it reaches customers. You will read requirements, write and run manual test cases, try the software the way real users do, and find the problems that automated checks miss. When you find a bug, you will report it clearly, with the steps to repeat it, so the developers can fix it quickly, and then check that the fix works and that nothing else has broken. You will also help improve how we test and keep the test cases up to date. We are looking for someone with a sharp eye for detail, patience and good written communication. Tell us about your testing experience and the kinds of software you have tested.

Design outfits that people love to wear, for every occasion. You will create original designs for weddings, festivals, parties, office and everyday wear, choosing the right fabric, colour, cut and finish for each occasion and for the person who will wear it.
